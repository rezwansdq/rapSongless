How to Play

The Goal

Guess the song from a short audio snippet. Every wrong guess or skip makes the next snippet longer.

Gameplay

  • Listen — each round opens with a fraction of a second of the track.
  • Guess — start typing and the search will suggest titles.
  • Skip — stuck? Skip to unlock a longer snippet.
  • Six stages — 0.2s, 0.7s, 2.5s, 5s, 9s, then 15s. Miss all six and the answer is revealed.

Daily Challenge

Every day brings 3 songs — one rap, one pop, one from the wider chart — the same three for everyone. Missed a day? Open Past Days for a calendar of the last 100 days; each one always has the songs it had on the day. Results are saved in this browser, so they're here when you come back.

Other Modes

Play endlessly from a single artist or a chart genre instead.
